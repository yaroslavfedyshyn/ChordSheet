## Description (4000 characters max)
Chord Sheet is a fast, distraction-free place to write and play from
your own chord sheets — lyrics with chords typed right above the words,
just like on paper.

FEATURES
• Chord-aware editor — chords are highlighted automatically as you type, so lyrics and chords stay easy to tell apart
• One-tap transpose — change key instantly without retyping a single chord
• Key detection — get a best-guess key from the chords you've already written
• Organize your way — tag songs, pin favorites, search your library, sort by name or date
• Adjustable text size for easy reading on stage or in rehearsal
• Light, dark, and automatic appearance, with a choice of accent colors
• Import and export your whole library as a backup file, anytime
• Works fully offline — your songs are stored only on your device
• Available in English, French, German, Spanish, Russian, and Ukrainian

Chord Sheet is a one-time purchase. No ads, no subscriptions, no
feature limits — buy once and everything is unlocked, forever.
